# Proof guide for the manuscript

Status: `VERIFIED` (Lean kernel checked), for the precise main-theorem
scope in [README](README.md). The publication entry point is
[`manuscriptMainTheorems`](CoefficientwiseRigidity/Paper.lean), an actual
proof of `ManuscriptMainTheorems`. [FORMALIZATION](FORMALIZATION.md) lists
auxiliary article results outside this proposition.

## Counting and normalization

`Definitions` defines a cover by inverse permutation transports. A
configuration chooses an actual finite label set of prescribed size at
each vertex; transported sets at adjacent vertices must be disjoint.
`coefficient` is the cardinality of this type. `deficit` uses integer
subtraction, so a negative difference is not silently truncated.

`Polynomial.ordinary_eq_polynomial_coefficient` identifies the ordinary
count with the coefficient of the usual independent-set polynomial raised
to M. `Lift.coefficient_eq_projected_lift` identifies the gained count with
the projected polynomial of the actual cover graph. Both use explicit
bijections; there is no factorial normalization.

The internal `reserve` uses natural subtraction and is intended for
positive occupations. `PaperReserve.paperReserve` sets it to zero when
some occupation is zero. `paperReserve_eq_reserve` proves equality on the
positive domain, and `paperReserve_eq_polynomial_coefficient` proves,
for every profile, the exact coefficient identity

    paperReserve(G,M,a) = [x^a](x^V p_G^(M-2)).

This handles the negative-exponent convention, M=2, and empty vertex sets.
`coefficient_zero_profile` proves the all-zero count is one on every cover;
in particular `reserve_singleton` is one.

`Gauge` proves count preservation under vertex relabelings. `Support`
deletes zero occupations using an actual induced vertex type and proves
the active-flatness correspondence. `Feasibility` proves that infeasible
profiles have zero counts and that ordinary counts on a bipartite base
are positive exactly at feasible profiles.

## Theorems A and B: the width-two route

`Targets.TreewidthAtMostTwo` is the standard tree-bag definition with
bags of size at most three and running intersection. `TreeBags` and
`TreeElimination` derive low-degree vertices and smoothing closure from
that definition. No comparison or elimination oracle defines the class.

`Newton`, `NewtonSplit` and `SerialClosure` establish finite rational
Newton-cone closure. The serial proof uses the independently proved
two-label cancellation in `SerialDifference`, including dense slices.
`GainKernels` retains all concrete gain lists and exact ordinary companions.
`NetworkTreewidth` packs the actual incident lists and proves scalar
comparison by vertex-count induction. `CoefficientNetwork` and
`NetworkGraph` identify its sum and graph with the original counting problem.

For the deficit, `MovedSubsets`, `KappaBounds` and `LocalDefect` establish
the moved-subset estimate with the original kappa, including the uniform
two and odd-group three bounds. The local Lean proof uses serial
monotonicity and two-label cancellation; it does not assume the paper's
trace expansion. The separate `Slice*`, `Incidence*`, `CommonScalars` and
`NewtonSpectral` modules prove the counting-normalized harmonic decomposition
and common scalar action on real spaces.

`KernelInsertion` through `NetworkReserveValue` identify the literal
two-fewer-label reserve. `NetworkExactPacking` and `NetworkLowDegree`
construct every degree-zero, leaf and serial presentation from actual
bags. `NetworkRigidStep`, `ParallelRigidity` and `NetworkBridge` handle
nonflat branches and the terminal bridge, including the both-flat branch.
`NetworkQuantitativeInduction` discharges the smaller-vertex hypotheses.
`CoefficientQuantitative` returns to the independent-set coefficient and
reserve, and `HolonomyGauge` gauges the original cover into its actual
root-walk holonomy group. These yield `mainBTwo`, `mainBKappa`, `mainBOdd`.
`CoefficientBoundary` includes small layer counts, empty support and full
isolated ranks in the width-two equality statement.

`Sharpness` proves two concrete C4 attainment examples by a bijection
between singleton configurations and finite legal colourings. Kernel
reduction, rather than native evaluation, proves P=2,Z=0 at M=2 and
P=18,Z=15 at M=3. It also proves connectedness, the actual two-bag
decomposition, nonflatness, and holonomy equal to the cyclic subgroup
generated by the twist. The second group has cardinality three.
Together with R=1, `paperMainBSharpness` witnesses optimality of both
uniform constants under every hypothesis of Theorem B.

`PolynomialBound.coefficient_kappa_paperReserve` extends the kappa bound
to every profile using the correctly shifted polynomial reserve. Zero
occupations and infeasible profiles are treated explicitly. This is the
coefficient form of `cor:polynomial`; activity evaluation is not part of
the publication target.

## Theorem A: the chordal-bipartite route

`Targets.ChordalBipartite` forbids actual induced cycles of length at least
six and explicitly requires bipartiteness. `PrimalChordal`,
`IncidenceConformal`, `ChordalSeparators` and `ChordalSimplicial` derive
the structural facts; `ChordalNestOrder` and `GraphNestOrder` construct
root-last orders in the original graph. The hereditary alpha-leaf premise
is proved from cycle exclusion.

`Compression*` prove simultaneous set compression. `IncreasingProductCone`,
`ConeCompression` and `ConeElimination` prove closure and comparison for
the full rational increasing product cone. `GraphConeComparison`,
`CoverIntersections` and `RootIntersections` identify original coefficients
and either-side root fibres at every rank.

`Saturation*`, `GraphSaturation` and `IntegerSaturation` prove an exact
integer expansion with nonnegative surplus and positive-multiplier
zero-deficit transfer. `LabelledCover`, `Star*`, `TightStar` and
`SaturatedStar` implement contraction, exact configuration maps, strict
vertex decrease, feasible ranks and induced-cycle lifting. All parallel
labels remain present. `RepresentativeCover`, `OrdinaryWitness`,
`ParallelRejection` and `IncidentStar` construct a global assignment
rejected by an omitted nonidentity constraint. `ChordalRigidity` closes
the vertex induction; `MainA.paperMainA` joins the two structural routes
with the positive-support bridge.

## Theorem C: actual seams and construction occurrences

`StructuralHull` permits only empty, vertex and actual-edge seams.
`SeamConfigurations` gives an exact pullback equivalence of configuration
types; `SeamNormalization` gives ordinary boundary constants.
`ReplicaConfigurations`, `BoundaryAmplification`, `ReplicaBags` and
`ReplicaChordal` prove atom fibre bounds from arbitrarily many actual
copies whose graph class is preserved. `AtomFibres` needs no all-copy
comparison premise. `HullComparison` retains any root or actual edge
through a construction. `HullFibres.paperMainCFibres` supplies the
normalization-free multiplication form of the fibre inequalities.

`SeamScalars` proves Z12 <= c2 Z1 and P12 = c2 P1, and the corresponding
right formulas. Feasibility supplies positive P and c. `HullRigidity`
forces equality in both children; `SeamFlatness` glues their active gauges.

`HullRelative.ConstructionPiece` tracks either an atom occurrence or a
whole intermediate subtree. Unlike `WitnessHull`, its `here` constructor
accepts any structural subtree. Dividing the actual scalar bounds by
positive ordinary counts proves the one-step relative-deficit inequality;
induction along either child proves the full occurrence statement.
`paperMainCRelative` removes the induced-universe wrapper.
`relativeDeficit_bounds` proves that each feasible relative deficit lies
in [0,1]. Ratios are rational exact counts, with their usual real embedding.

`SeamQuantitative` uses actual pinned ordinary insertion to compare the
original and reduced partner constants. `HullQuantitative` follows the
designated `WitnessHull` atom occurrence to the root and handles zero
reserve separately. Its arithmetic now works over any linear ordered
field. `paperMainCQuantitativeReal` specializes it to arbitrary positive
real lambda and to `paperReserve`; it uses no rational-density assumption.

## Verification and boundaries

The default `lake build` includes `PaperAudit`. It scans transitive axiom
dependencies of every safe declaration in the project namespace, including
definitions and final targets, and permits only `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound`. No project axiom, `sorryAx` or native-evaluation axiom is
accepted. The standalone validator described in [README](README.md#reproduce-the-proof)
records a clean rebuild, a fresh audit and independent finite diagnostics
in the ignored validation directory. [GUARDRAILS](GUARDRAILS.md) retains
useful refuted shortcuts. Finite diagnostics are `COMPUTATIONAL`
and are not proof premises.
